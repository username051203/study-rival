## Full description (4000 chars max)

Study Rival turns exam prep into a head-to-head match against Alicia, an AI
study rival who tracks her own progress alongside yours. Built for JEE and
NEET/MBBS aspirants who want a structured, gamified way to stay consistent.

FEATURES
• Daily/weekly study logging across your full syllabus
• Topic-by-topic mastery tracking so you always know what's weak
• Study streaks and session history
• A rival (Alicia) who progresses in parallel — see who's ahead
• Practice Quiz: paste any question, get similar practice questions
  generated instantly, answer against a timer, get auto-graded
  (requires your own free API key — see in-app setup)
• Cosmetics and progress rewards to keep motivation up
• Local backup/export — your data stays on your device
• Works fully offline (except the optional Practice Quiz feature)

WHO IT'S FOR
Students preparing for JEE (Main/Advanced) or NEET/MBBS entrance exams who
want more structure than a plain to-do list, without needing a subscription
or an account.

PRIVACY
No account required. No study data leaves your device unless you use the
optional AI practice-question feature, which only activates if you add your
own API key.
