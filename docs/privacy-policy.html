Privacy Policy — Study Rival

Last updated: August 2026

Study Rival is a self-study companion app. This page explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Data stored on your device

Your study logs, schedule, syllabus progress, streaks, and app settings are stored locally on your device (using Android's local database storage). This data is not uploaded to any server operated by the developer. It stays on your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself (e.g. via the app's backup/share feature).

Practice Quiz feature (optional)

The Practice Quiz feature is optional and requires you to provide your own API key from Groq (a third-party AI provider, groq.com). If you use this feature:

  • The questions you type and the practice questions generated are sent to Groq's servers to generate similar questions and grade your answers.
  • Your Groq API key is stored locally on your device only. The developer of Study Rival never receives or has access to it.
  • Use of this feature is subject to Groq's own privacy policy, since Groq — not the developer of Study Rival — processes that data.
  • If you don't use this feature, no data is sent anywhere for it.

Advertising

Study Rival shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to serve and measure ads, and may use this data for ad personalization in accordance with your device's ad settings. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

What the developer does not collect

Study Rival does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, or other personal identifiers, and does not sell any data. The developer has no server that stores your study data.

Children's privacy

Study Rival is not directed at children under 13. It is designed for students preparing for competitive exams (e.g. JEE, NEET) who are generally teenagers and above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as features change. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the GitHub repository's issue tracker.
